# Privacy policy

This policy covers the **Send to Katevazo** browser extension (Chrome, Edge and Firefox).
Last updated: 31 October 2026.

## The short version

The extension does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data, and it makes no
network requests of its own.

## What it does

The extension sends the page you are viewing, or a link you choose, to the Katevazo desktop
app on your own computer. It does this by opening a `katevazo://` address, which your
operating system hands to the app.

- When you click the toolbar button, it reads the address of the current tab, using the
  `activeTab` permission, and passes it to the app.
- When you use a right-click menu entry, it passes that page or link to the app. This uses
  the `contextMenus` permission.
- On `www.youtube.com` watch pages it adds a download button to the video player. The page
  script reads only the address of the video you are watching, and only when you click the
  button.

## What it does not do

- It does not collect browsing history, page content, account details or any other personal
  data.
- It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or remote code.
- It does not send anything to any server. The link goes only to the Katevazo app on your
  computer, which loads it so you can choose a format. Nothing downloads until you press
  Download in the app.
- It stores nothing, apart from what your browser keeps for any installed extension.
